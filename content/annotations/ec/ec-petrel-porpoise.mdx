The Battle of the Atlantic began on the first day of the war, 3 September 1939, when a U-boat sank the liner *Athenia*, and the vast waters of these lines were being fought over as Eliot wrote. The storm petrel ranges across the whole Atlantic. Ricks and McCue add two family connections: the navigator William Dampier, who explained the petrel's name, was born at East Coker, and Eliot's great-grandfather owned whaling ships out of New Bedford.
