A draft of the ending sent the explorer through three landscapes of the religious life: the forest of the Indian hermit-sages, the desert of the early Christian hermits, and the sea crossed by the early Irish missionaries. One version named the *Aranyaka*, the ‘forest books’ of Sanskrit scripture, which include the Upanishads. Only the sea survives in the final lines, with the petrel and the porpoise, and it leads straight into *The Dry Salvages*.
