The London Underground again, as in Burnt Norton III, but now at a standstill. When a train stops too long in the tunnel, conversation falters, and the passengers are left facing an emptiness they have nothing to fill with — the ‘growing terror’ of having nothing to think about. The theatre whose scenery is rolled away in the dark, and the patient conscious of nothing under ether, make the same point: the familiar world withdrawn, and the mind left without anything to hold.

What in Burnt Norton was a place of distraction becomes here a place where distraction fails, which is the beginning of something else.
