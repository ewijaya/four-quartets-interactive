Knowledge imposes a pattern on life and so falsifies it, because the pattern changes at every moment, and each moment revalues everything we have been. We are freed only from the illusions that can no longer hurt us. Like Dante lost in the dark wood at the middle of life's journey, the speaker has been lost the whole way: in brambles, at the edge of a bog, among monsters and false lights. Let us hear not about old men's wisdom but about their folly and fear, including the fear of belonging to anyone, or to God. Humility, which is endless, is the one wisdom within our reach.
