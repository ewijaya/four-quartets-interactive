A brief lyrical interruption: running water, a flash of lightning in winter, wild thyme and wild strawberries hidden in the grass, laughter in the garden. These moments of joy recall the children in the garden of *Burnt Norton*. They are not lost, but they are not the goal either; they point beyond themselves, to the agony of dying and being born.
