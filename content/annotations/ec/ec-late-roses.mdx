The disordered seasons had a basis in fact. November 1938 was unusually warm in England, with temperatures near 70°F, and snow fell in the week before Christmas. The rose filled with snow recalls Thomas Campion's song ‘There is a garden in her face’, where the lady's lips are rosebuds filled with snow. It also carries the rose of the *Burnt Norton* garden into a winter that has come too soon.
