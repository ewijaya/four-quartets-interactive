The sky becomes a battlefield of the zodiac: the Scorpion against the Sun, triumphal chariots of thunder, comets and the Leonids — the meteor shower that appears each year in mid-November, which fixes the lyric in its month. Written in the first winter of the Second World War, the ‘constellated wars’ read as the human war projected onto the stars.

The ending looks back to old cosmologies in which the world is periodically destroyed by fire, and forward to an age of ice. The apocalyptic language is grand, and that grandeur is what the next lines reject as a worn-out fashion.
