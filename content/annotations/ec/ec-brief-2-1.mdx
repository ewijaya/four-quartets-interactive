A rhymed lyric about a world out of joint. Late November is full of the disturbances of spring and summer: snowdrops underfoot, hollyhocks reaching too high and falling, late roses filled with early snow. The disorder spreads to the heavens. Thunder rolls like the chariots of a triumph in a war of the constellations; the Scorpion fights the Sun; comets and November meteors hunt across the sky; everything is whirled towards a world-ending fire, which will be followed by the reign of ice.
