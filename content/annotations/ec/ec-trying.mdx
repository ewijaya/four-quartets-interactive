Perhaps it is neither gain nor loss. What remains to us is ‘only the trying’; the outcome is none of our business. The line lets go of any claim on results. It anticipates The Dry Salvages III, where Krishna's counsel from the Bhagavad Gita — act, but without thought for the fruits of action — is set at the heart of the poem, and the close of The Dry Salvages, where we are ‘undefeated’ only because we have gone on trying.
