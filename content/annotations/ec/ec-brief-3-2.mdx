But the speaker tells his soul to be still and let the darkness come, because it will be the darkness of God. Three images show what that darkness is like: a theatre where the lights go out while the scenery is changed; an Underground train stopped between stations, where talk dies and you see fear of emptiness behind every face; and anaesthesia, when the mind is conscious of nothing. So the soul must wait without hope, love or thought, since these would fasten on the wrong things; faith, hope and love are all in the waiting. Then the dark will be light and the stillness a dance.
