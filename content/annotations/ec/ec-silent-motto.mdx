An arras is a wall-hanging tapestry, named after the Flemish town where the finest were woven. This one is old and torn, shaken by the wind in an empty house, and it bears a motto no one reads any more. The poem leaves the motto unstated, but it is hard not to hear the one that frames the movement: the words woven into a queen's cloth of state, now surviving only as a fragment in a ruin.
