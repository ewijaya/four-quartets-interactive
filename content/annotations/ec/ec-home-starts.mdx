A proverb bent into a new shape. The familiar saying makes home a place of the heart; this one makes it a point of departure. For Eliot — born in St Louis, a descendant of East Coker, a British subject since 1927 — the line has a personal edge.

As we grow older the pattern grows more complicated, and the ‘intense moment’ of Burnt Norton is no longer enough: what matters is a lifetime, and not one person's lifetime but that of generations whose gravestones can no longer be read.
