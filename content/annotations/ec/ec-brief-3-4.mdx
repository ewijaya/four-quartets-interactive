The speaker admits he is repeating himself, and will again. The lines that follow adapt a set of paradoxes from St John of the Cross. To reach the place where you already are, you must travel a road with no ecstasy in it; to reach what you are ignorant of, the road of ignorance; to own what you lack, the road of giving everything away; to become something new, the road through what you are not yet. The conclusions sound like riddles: your ignorance is your only knowledge, what you own is not yours, and you are not really where you are.
