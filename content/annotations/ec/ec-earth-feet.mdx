The verse slows into the rhythm of the dance it describes. Stressed monosyllables are set side by side — earth, feet, loam, feet — so that the line cannot be read quickly; each word is a footfall. The echo of ‘mirth’ and ‘earth’ across the lines ties the dancers' laughter to the ground where they now lie, and ‘feet’ carries its metrical sense as well as its bodily one.
