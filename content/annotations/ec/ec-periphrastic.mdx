An abrupt change of register. The poet looks back at the lyric he has just written and finds it wanting: a roundabout way of saying something, in a style that no longer serves. ‘The poetry does not matter’ is a startling sentence for a poet to write in a poem, and it announces what the rest of the quartet is concerned with — not beautiful effects but the ‘intolerable wrestle’ with language and meaning.

This self-correction is part of the design of every quartet's second movement, but here it is sharpest. It anticipates the long complaint about words in the fifth movement.
