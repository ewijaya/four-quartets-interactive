Home is where one sets out from, and as we grow older the world grows stranger, the pattern of the dead and the living more complicated. What matters is not the isolated intense moment but a whole life burning in every moment, and not one life only but the lives of the dead under old, illegible gravestones. There is a time for evenings under the stars and a time for evenings by lamplight with the family photographs. Love is most fully itself when the here and the now no longer matter.
