The vision fades at dawn, as ghosts do. Another day of heat and silence is beginning, and out at sea the dawn wind ruffles the water. The speaker steps back from the scene and from himself: he is here, or elsewhere, anywhere. The movement ends on the first half of its motto, left unfinished.
