For thirty years American editions printed ‘Here and there’, from the first American *Four Quartets* (1943) until 1974; the critic Hermann Peschmann pointed out the error. Eliot said in 1950 that he preferred ‘or’: the point is not that the explorer goes to many places, but that it does not matter where he is. The line answers the end of the first movement, where the speaker is here, or there, or elsewhere.
