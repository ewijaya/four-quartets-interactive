J. W. N. Sullivan's *Beethoven: His Spiritual Development* (1927) describes the late Beethoven as reconciling assertion and submission, and ‘submission’ is a word from Beethoven's own journal. Eliot had the late quartets in mind while writing the sequence. The pairing names the double effort of the artist: force of will, and a yielding to what is there to be found. What there is to conquer has already been discovered by greater men; the task now is to recover it.
