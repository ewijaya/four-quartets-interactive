The roll-call of the eminent going into the dark recalls Revelation 6:15, where at the opening of the sixth seal the kings of the earth, the great men, the rich men and the chief captains hide in the dens and rocks of the mountains. Eliot had mocked the City's faith in money before: a chorus drafted for *The Rock* (1934) parodied the last line of Dante's *Paradiso*, putting dividends in place of the love that moves the sun and stars. When a correspondent asked in 1940, he insisted that the dark these men enter is not the mystic's dark night of the soul.
