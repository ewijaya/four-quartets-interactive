Old men should be explorers, wherever they happen to be. We must stay still and yet keep moving, into a new intensity and a deeper union, through cold and desolation, the cries of wind and wave, and the vast seas of the petrel and the porpoise. The movement ends by turning its opening motto the right way round: in my end is my beginning. The sea at the close looks forward to *The Dry Salvages*.
