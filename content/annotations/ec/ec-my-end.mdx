The last words restore Mary Stuart's motto to its original order: in my end is my beginning. The poem opened with the inverted form — every beginning contains its end — and closes with the Christian form, in which the end opens on something new.

The phrase has a second life outside the poem. Both versions are inscribed on Eliot's memorial tablet in St Michael's church, East Coker, where his ashes were placed in 1965.
