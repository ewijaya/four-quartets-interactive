The motto again, and a present scene: an afternoon in late summer at East Coker. Light falls across the open field, but the sunken lane is dark under its branches; you step aside against the bank as a van goes by, and the lane leads you into the village, dazed by thundery heat. The grey stone soaks up the hazy light instead of throwing it back, the dahlias sleep in the silence, and all that is left is to wait for the first owl of evening.
