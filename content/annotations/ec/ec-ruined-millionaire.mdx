The world is a hospital endowed by a ruined benefactor. The usual reading makes him Adam, who was given everything and lost it, and whose loss is the inheritance on which the hospital runs. If all goes well, we will die of the ‘absolute paternal care’ — God's providence, which ‘prevents’ us in the old sense of the word: goes before us, anticipates us, as in the Book of Common Prayer collect that asks God to prevent us in all our doings.
