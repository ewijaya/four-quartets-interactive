A brief lyric interlude in the darkness: running water, winter lightning, wild thyme and wild strawberry, laughter in the garden. The laughter recalls the hidden children of Burnt Norton; the ecstasy is ‘not lost’, but it points beyond itself to death and birth. Several of these images return in The Dry Salvages V as examples of the ‘unattended moment’ — the wild thyme unseen, the winter lightning — so that the three quartets share a small store of remembered moments.
