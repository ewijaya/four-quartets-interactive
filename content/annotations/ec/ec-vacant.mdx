Pascal's most famous sentence confesses his terror at the eternal silence of infinite space; Eliot had written an introduction to Pascal's *Pensées* in 1931. Here the spaces between the stars are empty, and the eminent men go into them: the vacant into the vacant. Eliot claimed in 1953 that astronomy bored him, and the cosmic scale interests the poem only as a measure of human emptiness.
