The procession into the dark is a satire on worldly importance: captains, merchant bankers, men of letters, patrons, statesmen, civil servants, committee chairmen. Then the reference books that record them go dark too. The *Almanach de Gotha* was the annual register of Europe's royal and noble families, published at Gotha from the eighteenth century; the *Stock Exchange Gazette* and the *Directory of Directors* were the equivalent registers of the financial world.

Eliot knew that world from the inside: he worked in the foreign department of Lloyds Bank from 1917 to 1925 before joining the publishers Faber and Gwyer.
