The paradoxes adapt St John of the Cross, who teaches that the soul must go to God through what it does not know. William James quoted the same passage in *The Varieties of Religious Experience* (1902), which Eliot read as a young man; E. Allison Peers's English translation of St John appeared in 1934–35. Eliot said in 1962 that the lines were based on St John's prose rather than his verse. Ricks and McCue add Pascal's praise of a learned ignorance, the knowledge of what cannot be known.
