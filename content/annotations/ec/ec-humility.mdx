The one wisdom the poem allows is the admission of ignorance. What the old should show us is not their serenity but their folly: their fear of losing control, of belonging to another person, to others, or to God. Humility is ‘endless’ both because it is never finished and because it is the only kind of wisdom with no limit. The third movement follows this through to its extreme, in a way of dispossession borrowed from St John of the Cross.
