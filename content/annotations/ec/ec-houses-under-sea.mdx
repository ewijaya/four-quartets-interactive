Two closing lines gather up the first movement: its houses and its dancers are all gone — the houses under the sea, the dancers under the hill. ‘Under the hill’ suggests the churchyard, and also the old belief in fairy folk who live inside hollow hills; the dancers were a vision to begin with. The houses under the sea look ahead to the next quartet, where the sea swallows the works of men and tosses back their wreckage.
