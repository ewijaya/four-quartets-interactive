The quartet ends not on the vision but on what surrounds it: ordinary time, before and after the moment, seen as waste. ‘Ridiculous’ is a surprising, deflating word — the wasted time is absurd as well as sad. East Coker V will take up the theme of wasted years in a more personal key.
