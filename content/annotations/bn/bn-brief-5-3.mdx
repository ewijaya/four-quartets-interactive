The detail of any pattern is movement, as in the mystic's ladder of ten steps. Desire is movement, and is not worth desiring for its own sake. Love, by contrast, does not move: like Aristotle's unmoved mover, it is the cause and goal of all movement. In itself it is outside time and without desire, and it takes the shape of desire only as it appears in time, caught in the limited form of creatures who hover between non-being and being.
