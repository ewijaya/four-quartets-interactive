The Chimera is the fire-breathing hybrid monster of Greek myth, and by extension any wild illusion. In Flaubert's *The Temptation of Saint Anthony* (1874) — a hermit tempted by visions in the desert — the Chimera appears near the end in dialogue with the Sphinx. The association with a desert temptation makes Flaubert a plausible background, though not a certain one. Its loud lament joins the shrieking and chattering voices that assail the Word.
