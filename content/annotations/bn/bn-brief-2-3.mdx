What that experience is like. It frees us from wanting and from having to act, from pressures inside and out, and yet it is not empty: it comes as a grace felt through the senses, a white light both still and moving. The soul is raised without moving; it concentrates without shutting anything out. It finds a new world that is also the old world made clear, and it understands both the ecstasy and the horror of ordinary experience, which were only partial, by seeing them completed.
