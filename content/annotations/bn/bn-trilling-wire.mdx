The lyric listens inward: the blood sings like a vibrating wire, beneath old wounds that have hardened into scars. The body's rhythms — pulse, circulation — are heard as music, and in the following lines are matched with the movements of the heavens. Pain is not denied here; it is set within a larger harmony.
