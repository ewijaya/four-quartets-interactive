St John of the Cross, in *The Dark Night*, describes ten steps of a ‘mystic ladder of love’ by which the soul climbs to God. The poem invokes the figure as a pattern of movement: the ascent is a succession of steps, a detail of movement, while its source and goal — love — does not move. The mystic's ladder complements the descent of the third movement: the way up and the way down.
