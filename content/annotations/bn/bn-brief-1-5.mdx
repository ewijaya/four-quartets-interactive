The bird sends them away. The leaves are full of hidden children, trying not to laugh, but the moment has passed, and the bird's reason is blunt: human beings cannot bear very much reality. The movement closes by repeating its opening proposition. Past and future, what might have been and what has been, all point to the one end that is always present.
