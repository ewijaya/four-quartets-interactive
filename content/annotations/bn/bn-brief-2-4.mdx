Yet we live in bodies bound to past and future, and that bondage protects us from both heaven and damnation, which flesh could not endure. We can bear very little awareness. To be fully conscious would be to stand outside time, and yet the great moments of a life can only be remembered within time: the rose-garden, an arbour in the rain, a draughty church at nightfall. The paradox closes the movement: time can be overcome only by living through it.
