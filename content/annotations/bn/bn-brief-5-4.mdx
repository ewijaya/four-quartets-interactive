The garden returns. Suddenly, in a shaft of sunlight, while the dust is still moving, the hidden laughter of the children in the leaves rises again: the moment of the first movement, given now as a present possibility. The line that follows insists on it: now, here, always. Against that moment the rest of time, stretching before and after it, looks absurd and sad, a waste.
