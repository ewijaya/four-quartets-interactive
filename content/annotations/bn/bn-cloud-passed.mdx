The vision ends as simply as it began: the sun goes in, the shimmer stops, and the pool is an empty pool again. The flatness of the statement is deliberate. Nothing is explained; the experience is not claimed as revelation. It is simply over — which is why the next lines turn to how little reality human beings can bear.
