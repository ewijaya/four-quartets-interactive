Three parallel phrases name a threefold emptying: of the senses, of the imagination (‘fancy’), and of the spirit's own activity. St John of the Cross describes a ‘night of sense’ followed by a ‘night of spirit’; the poem's list follows that order, moving from the body inward. The abstract Latinate nouns — desiccation, evacuation, inoperancy — themselves feel drained of colour.
