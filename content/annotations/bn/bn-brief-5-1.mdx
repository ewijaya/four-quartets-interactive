Words and music move only in time, and whatever only lives must die. Yet words, once spoken, reach into silence, and through form and pattern they can touch stillness, as a Chinese jar seems to keep moving while standing still. That stillness is not like a single sustained note on a violin. It is more like a co-existence in which the end comes before the beginning, and beginning and end are always there together, so that everything is always now.
