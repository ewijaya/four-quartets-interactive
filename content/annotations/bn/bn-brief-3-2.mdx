Neither full nor empty, the place holds only a flicker over strained faces, ruled by the clock, distracted from their distractions by more distraction, full of fancies and empty of meaning. People and litter are blown along by the cold wind of a tunnel before a train, a wind that blows through time and through unhealthy lungs. The station breathes out its souls into the dull air of London's hills, from Hampstead to Ludgate. The true darkness, the speaker says, is not here, in this chattering half-world.
