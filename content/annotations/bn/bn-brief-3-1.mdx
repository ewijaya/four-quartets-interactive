The scene shifts to the London Underground, a place of disaffection in every sense: estranged, discontented, cut off from feeling. Its dim light is neither one thing nor the other. It is not the daylight that gives things clear form and makes passing shadows beautiful and seemingly lasting. Nor is it the darkness that purifies the soul, stripping the senses and cleansing love of its attachment to temporary things.
