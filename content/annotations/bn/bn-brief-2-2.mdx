The centre of the whole sequence, described almost entirely by what it is not. At the still point of the turning world there is neither flesh nor its absence, neither movement nor fixed rest, neither rising nor falling; past and future are gathered there. Without that point there would be no dance, and yet the dance is all there is. The speaker can say that ‘we’ have been there, but not where, and not for how long, because to measure it would put it back into time.
