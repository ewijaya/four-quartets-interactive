The visitors and the presences move together in a formal pattern, like figures in a dance, along the alley and into the circle of box hedges, and look down into the empty pool. For a moment the dry concrete basin seems full of water made of sunlight, a lotus rises, the surface shines, and the unseen company is reflected behind them. Then a cloud crosses the sun and the pool is only an empty pool again. The vision is given in the plainest terms, and it lasts only a moment.
