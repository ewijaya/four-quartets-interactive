Dance is the sequence's figure for movement that is also pattern — freedom and form at once. Without the still point the dance could not exist; yet the dance is all there is to experience. John Hayward traced the image to the dance of the Tarot figures round a still centre in Charles Williams's novel *The Greater Trumps* (1932), and Eliot mentioned the novel to Helen Gardner too. The figure grows through the quartets: the rustic dancers of East Coker I keeping time with the seasons, and in Little Gidding V the right arrangement of words as a ‘consort’ of instruments and dancers moving together.
