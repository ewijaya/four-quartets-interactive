The argument turns to memory. We hear, as if in memory, footsteps along a passage we never took, towards a door we never opened, into a rose-garden: the image of a happiness that was possible and missed. The poet notes that his words now echo in the reader's mind as those footsteps echo in his, and then admits he does not know why he is stirring up this old dust, like the dust on a bowl of dried rose petals.
