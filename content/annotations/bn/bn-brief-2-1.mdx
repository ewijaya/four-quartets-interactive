A rhymed lyric, close to incantation. It joins the lowest things to the highest: garlic and jewels stuck in the mud of a cart's axle; the song in the blood beneath old scars; the dance of the pulse and the flow of body fluids, which mirror the drifting stars. We are lifted up with the sap of the tree into the light on its leaves, while below, on the wet ground, the hunt of the boar goes on as it always has. But even that old conflict is reconciled, seen as part of the pattern of the stars.
