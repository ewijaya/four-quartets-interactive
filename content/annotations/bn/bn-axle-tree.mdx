An axle-tree is the fixed bar on which a wheel turns — an old term from carts and carriages. Clogged with mud and jewels, it cannot turn: the image anticipates the still point, the unmoving centre on which movement depends. ‘Bedded’ suggests both something sunk in mud and something laid to rest.
