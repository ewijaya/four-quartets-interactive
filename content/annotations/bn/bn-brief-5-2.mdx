But words are frail. They crack and break under the strain of meaning, slip and decay, and will not stay where they are put. Voices shriek at them, scold, mock and chatter. Even the Word, Christ in the wilderness, was attacked by the voices of temptation, and the poet's words are beset in the same way by the shadowy mourner at a funeral and by the wailing of a mind's fantasy, the disconsolate chimera.
