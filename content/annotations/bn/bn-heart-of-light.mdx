The phrase recalls the ‘hyacinth garden’ passage in the first part of *The Waste Land* (1922), where the speaker, looking into ‘the heart of light’, knows nothing and is lost in silence. There the moment of intensity ends in paralysis; here, a garden again, it is briefly radiant. The echo links Burnt Norton's rose-garden with the earlier poem's failed moment of love. John Hayward also heard Dante: in *Paradiso* XII a voice speaks from the heart of one of the new lights.
