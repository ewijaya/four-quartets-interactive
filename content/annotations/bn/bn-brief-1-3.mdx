Other echoes live in the garden, and a bird urges the speaker to follow them round the corner and through the gate into ‘our first world’, the unspoiled world of childhood. The bird may be deceiving him. Invisible presences move there over the dead leaves in the heat of early autumn, and the bird's song answers a music no one can hear. Eliot told a translator that these presences are the traces left in the garden by people of former times who had known it.
