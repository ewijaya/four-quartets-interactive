Horace's ode to Dellius (*Odes* II.3) begins with the advice to keep an equal mind in hard times, and Dryden has ‘With equal Mind’ in his *Palamon and Arcite*. The deeper source is the *Bhagavad Gita*, where yoga is defined as evenness of mind: acting without attachment, alike in success and failure. Gardner notes that Eliot took from the Gita this idea of disinterested action, not its metaphysics of rebirth. The moment neither of action nor of inaction is the Gita's action in inaction.
