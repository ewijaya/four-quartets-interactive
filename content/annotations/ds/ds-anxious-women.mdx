The women lying awake are the wives and mothers of Gloucester fishermen, whose widows Eliot had written about in 1928. Their efforts to unweave, unwind and unravel the past recall Penelope in the *Odyssey*, who unpicked each night the shroud she had woven by day to keep her suitors waiting for her husband's return. Their time is the anxious, calculating time of hope and fear, set against the bell's time, which is not ours.
