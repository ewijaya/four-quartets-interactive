The drowned end their voyage on the sand, at the water's edge, or in the depths that will not give them up. Ricks and McCue note Harriet Monroe's ‘The Ocean Liner’ (1914), where the sea has a million lips and laughs in its throat. Monroe edited the Chicago magazine *Poetry*, which in 1915 published ‘The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock’.
