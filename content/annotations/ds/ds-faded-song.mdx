The future can be as sentimental as the past: a faded tune, a pressed flower, a regret felt in advance for people not yet born, kept between the yellowed pages of an unopened book. Ricks and McCue recall the smell of lavender and dried rose leaves that hangs over Ibsen's *Hedda Gabler*, and Shakespeare's Sonnet 73, where the poet's age is a time of yellow leaves. Eliot said the capitalised Rose here refers to Charles I, the Royal Martyr.
