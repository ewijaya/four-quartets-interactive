For most of us there is only the ‘unattended / Moment’: the moments the earlier quartets remembered, here gathered into a list — the distraction lost in a shaft of sunlight (Burnt Norton), the wild thyme unseen and the winter lightning (East Coker), the waterfall. The last example is music heard so deeply that it is not heard at all: you become the music while it lasts.

These are ‘only hints and guesses’. The rest — prayer, observance, discipline, thought and action — is the slow work the saint's life is made of.
