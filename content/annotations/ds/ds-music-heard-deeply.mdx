Most of us have only the ‘unattended / Moment’: the moments the earlier quartets remembered, here gathered into a list — the distraction lost in a shaft of sunlight (Burnt Norton), the wild thyme unseen and the winter lightning (East Coker), the waterfall. The last example is music listened to so intently that it ceases to be heard: while it lasts, you are the music.

These are ‘only hints and guesses’. The rest — prayer, observance, discipline, thought and action — is the slow work the saint's life is made of.
