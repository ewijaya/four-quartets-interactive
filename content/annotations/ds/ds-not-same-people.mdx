The travellers are not the people who left the station, nor the people who will arrive. The idea of the self as a series of changing states goes back to Eliot's doctoral thesis on the philosopher F. H. Bradley, *Knowledge and Experience*, and returns in *The Cocktail Party* (1949), where to meet anyone is to meet a stranger. In a lecture on Valéry in 1946 Eliot said that a journey alters the place one set out from.
