A satirical catalogue of ways to read the future or probe the hidden: talking with Martians and spirits, reporting sightings of sea monsters, casting horoscopes, reading entrails or crystal balls, palms and fingers, lots and tea leaves, cards and magic stars; sedative drugs; and the dissecting of dreams in search of buried terrors. All these are common pastimes and drugs, and staples of the newspapers, and they flourish most in times of national distress, in Asia or in a London street.
