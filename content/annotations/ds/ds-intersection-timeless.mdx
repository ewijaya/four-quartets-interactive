To apprehend the point where the timeless intersects with time is ‘an occupation for the saint’ — and then, correcting itself, not an occupation at all but something given and taken in a lifetime of self-surrender. The geometrical image — a line crossing a plane at a single point — is a new form of Burnt Norton's still point. The next paragraph names what it is: the Incarnation.

Little Gidding takes up the phrase in its first movement, where the intersection of the timeless moment is ‘England and nowhere’.
