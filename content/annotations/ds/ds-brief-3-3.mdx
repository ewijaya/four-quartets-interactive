At nightfall a voice sings in the rigging and the wireless aerial, heard neither by the ear nor in any language. It tells the voyagers that they are not really travelling: between this shore and the far one, while time is suspended, they should look on past and future with the same even mind. At the moment beyond action and inaction they can receive Krishna's teaching that a man goes wherever his mind is fixed when he dies. Since any moment may be the moment of dying, that fixing of the mind is the one action which will bear fruit in the lives of others. Give no thought to the results of action; fare forward.
