The draft had ‘soothing menace’; Eliot changed it after Geoffrey Faber pointed out how many words in the passage ended in -ing. John Hayward queried the old-fashioned inversion ‘together heard’ a line above, which Ricks and McCue trace to Milton's ‘Lycidas’, and Eliot kept it. In line 36 the silent fog's ‘stillness’ became its ‘oppression’, again after a query from Faber. The passage was revised word by word to make its sounds exact.
