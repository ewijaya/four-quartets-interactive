The river's history in three stages — frontier, conveyor of commerce, problem for engineers — is the history of St Louis itself: a fur-trading post at the edge of the United States, a steamboat port, then an industrial city. The Eads Bridge, which carried the railway across the Mississippi at St Louis from 1874, was one of the great engineering feats of the century, in the city where Eliot grew up. The line need not refer to any one bridge; the point is that solving the problem made the river invisible.
