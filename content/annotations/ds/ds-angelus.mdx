The Angelus is a Catholic devotion recalling the Annunciation and the Incarnation, said three times a day — morning, noon and evening — at the ringing of a church bell. The sea bell that tolls over the drowned becomes a ‘perpetual angelus’: an unending call to prayer, and a reminder of the ‘one Annunciation’ with which the second movement ended.
