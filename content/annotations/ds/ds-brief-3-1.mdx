The poet wonders whether this is what Krishna meant in the *Bhagavad-Gita*, or one way of saying it: the future can be as sentimental as the past, a faded song or a sprig of pressed flowers, a regret felt on behalf of people not yet born, kept in a book never opened. As Heraclitus said, the road up and the road down are one; so are the road forward and the road back. The hard truth is that time does not heal: the patient who was hurt is no longer there.
