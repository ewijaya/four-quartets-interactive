The river is inside us, the sea all around us. The sea is also the edge of the land, the granite it reaches into, the beaches where it throws up hints of older forms of life: starfish, horseshoe crabs, a whale's backbone. In its rock pools it shows the curious child delicate seaweeds and anemones. It also throws up what we have lost: a torn net, a broken lobster pot, an oar, the gear of drowned foreign sailors. The sea has many voices and many gods.
