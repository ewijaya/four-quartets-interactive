In the Buddhist *Dhammapada*, the wise man is one for whom there is neither this shore nor the farther shore: desire and its end are pictured as two banks of a river. The voyagers are suspended between them while time is withdrawn, and are asked to hold past and future in balance. At Harvard Eliot spent two years studying Sanskrit, and Indian philosophy, with Charles Lanman and James Woods.
