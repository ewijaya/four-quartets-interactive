For most of us there are only unlooked-for moments, in and out of time: a lapse of attention in a shaft of sunlight, wild thyme in the grass, lightning in winter, a waterfall, music heard so deeply that you become the music while it lasts. These are no more than hints and guesses; the rest is prayer and observance, discipline, thinking and acting. The hint only half guessed, the gift only half understood, is the Incarnation: God's entry into time.
