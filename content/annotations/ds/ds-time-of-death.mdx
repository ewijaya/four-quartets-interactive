The quoted words paraphrase the Gita (VIII.6): whatever state of being a person's mind is set upon at the moment of death, to that state they go. Eliot adds a parenthesis that transforms it: every moment is the time of death. If each moment is the moment of death, every moment is decisive, and what the mind is intent upon now is the one action that will ‘fructify in the lives of others’.
