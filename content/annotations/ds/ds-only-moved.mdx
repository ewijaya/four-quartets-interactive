Without the Incarnation, action would be only the motion of a thing pushed from outside, with no source of movement in itself. The language inverts Aristotle's unmoved mover, the first cause that moves all things without being moved: here is the opposite pole, the merely moved, driven by blind powers. Burnt Norton's still point is the other side of the same thought.
