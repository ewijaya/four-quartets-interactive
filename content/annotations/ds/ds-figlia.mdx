‘Daughter of your Son’: the opening of St Bernard's prayer to the Virgin at the beginning of the last canto of Dante's *Paradiso*. Eliot's drafts and early printings had *suo* for *tuo*, a slip for the right word. The paradox — the mother is the daughter of her own child, since her son is also her creator — is of a piece with the other paradoxes of the sequence. It is the first time the quartets quote the *Paradiso*, whose closing images return at the very end of Little Gidding.
