For most of us that freedom is never fully reached here. We are undefeated only because we have kept trying, and we will be content at the end if our bodies, returned to the earth in time and not far from the churchyard yew, feed the life of soil that means something. Eliot said the poem begins where he began, with the Mississippi, and ends where he and his wife expected to end, at a village church in Somerset.
