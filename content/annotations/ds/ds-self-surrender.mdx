The saint's gift is received in a lifetime's dying into love. Eliot had used the language of surrender long before, of the poet: ‘Tradition and the Individual Talent’ (1919) describes the artist's progress as a continual giving up of himself, an extinction of personality. The phrase now describes a religious life rather than an artistic discipline, and the two are not far apart in the sequence.
