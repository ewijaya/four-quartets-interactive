The fourth movement is a prayer to the Virgin Mary, Star of the Sea, patron of sailors. Commentators identify the shrine with the church of Our Lady of Good Voyage in Gloucester, built by the town's Portuguese fishing community, whose façade holds a statue of the Virgin cradling a schooner between two bell-towers above the harbour.

The prayer is for all who go to sea and all who wait on shore: fishermen, merchant sailors, women whose sons and husbands did not return, and the drowned who lie beyond the reach of the sea bell.
