The lyric turns to the fishermen who sail out into the tail of the wind and the fog. We cannot imagine a time without the ocean, an ocean without wreckage, or a future any more likely than the past to reach a destination. So we must think of the fishermen as always at their work, bailing, setting and hauling nets under a threatening sky, not as making a last voyage that will never pay. The final stanza gathers the refrains: no end of the wailing, the withering, the drifting, the bone's prayer to death. Only one prayer, Mary's answer at the Annunciation, can barely be prayed.
