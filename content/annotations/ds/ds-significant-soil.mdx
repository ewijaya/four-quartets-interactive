The quartet ends quietly, with burial. We are content if our ‘temporal reversion’ — a legal term for property returning to its original owner, here the body returning to earth — nourishes the soil, not too far from the churchyard yew. The dancers of East Coker, ‘nourishing the corn’, had already made this return.

‘Significant’ carries its full sense: soil that signifies, that means something, because it lies near the church and belongs to a place of prayer. It is a modest ending, and the modesty is the point.
