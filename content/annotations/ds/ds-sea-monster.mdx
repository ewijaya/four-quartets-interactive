The Loch Ness monster had been in the newspapers since 1933. Eliot first wrote of its ‘appearance’ and changed the word to ‘behaviour’. The list that follows is a satire of the occult pastimes that flourish in anxious times, and Eliot knew the field: Faber published books on astrology and the paranormal, and he read some of them for the firm. In 1942 *The Times* carried letters on the popularity of astrology in wartime.
