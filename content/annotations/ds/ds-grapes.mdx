Eliot remembered the smell of Concord grapes on the table when the family came back to St Louis each autumn from their summers at Gloucester, on the Massachusetts coast. The two landscapes of his boyhood, river and sea, meet in this quartet. He later said that it begins where he began, with the Mississippi, and ends where he and his wife expected to end, in a Somerset churchyard.
