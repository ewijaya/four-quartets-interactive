Curiosity has an older, darker sense: prying inquisitiveness, the wish to know what one has no business knowing. The word appeared in the first movement too, where the sea offers its rock pools to our curiosity. There it was a child's delight; here it is the anxious search of the fortune-tellers, clinging to past and future, set against the saint's attention to the timeless.
