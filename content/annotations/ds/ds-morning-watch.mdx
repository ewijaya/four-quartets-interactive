At sea the day is divided into watches. The morning watch runs from four to eight in the morning, so the time ‘before the morning watch’ is the dead of night, between midnight and four — the hours when the past seems all deception and the future futureless, and when time both stops and never ends. A watch is also what the women on shore are keeping.
