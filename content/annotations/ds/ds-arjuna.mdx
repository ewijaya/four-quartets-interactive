The Gita is set on the battlefield of Kurukshetra, between two armies of one family. Arjuna, seeing his teachers and kinsmen in the opposing ranks, lays down his bow; Krishna persuades him to fight, as his duty requires, without desire or hatred.

The poem was written in 1940, in the first year of a war. The last lines turn the counsel to the voyagers of the poem — ‘not fare well, / But fare forward’ — and to readers who, like Arjuna, must act in a war not of their choosing.
