The lyric speaks of three annunciations. The first is ‘calamitous’: news of disaster, the message that a boat has not come home. The second is ‘the last annunciation’, the bell that announces death. The third, in the last line, is capitalised: the one Annunciation, the angel Gabriel's message to Mary in Luke's Gospel, which announces the Incarnation.

The bone on the beach offers a prayer that is ‘unprayable’; only the last prayer is ‘hardly, barely prayable’. Between them the lyric moves from the endless drift of loss to a single, barely possible point of meaning — which the fourth and fifth movements take up.
