The first half of a strict lyric in six-line stanzas, a variant of the sestina, in which the same rhyme sounds return. It asks where there is an end to silent grief: flowers that wither without a sound, wreckage drifting on the sea, the bone on the beach whose prayer cannot be prayed. The answer is that there is no end, only addition: more days of living among the ruins of what we trusted most, and at the last the failing of our powers, a devotion that no longer feels like devotion, a slowly leaking boat, and the bell announcing death.
