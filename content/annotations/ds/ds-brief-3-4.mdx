The voice addresses sailors directly: those who reach port and those who will be judged by the sea. Whatever happens to them, this, the state of mind in which they act, is their real destination. The poet names his source: so Krishna spoke to the warrior Arjuna on the battlefield. The movement ends by turning a farewell into a command. Not ‘fare well’, but ‘fare forward’.
