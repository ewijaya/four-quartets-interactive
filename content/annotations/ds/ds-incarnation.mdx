The hint half guessed and the gift half understood have a name: ‘Incarnation’. The capitalised word is the Christian doctrine that God became man in Christ — the point where the timeless enters time. Here, the poem says, an impossible union of different orders of being becomes actual, and past and future are overcome and reconciled.

The line turns the ‘hints and guesses’ just described into their source: every moment of illumination is a small image of the one intersection.
