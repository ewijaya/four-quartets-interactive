The sea gives back what it has taken. The torn seine, the smashed lobster pot and the broken oar are the gear of the Gloucester fishery; near the Eliots' summer house there was a field where the nets were spread for mending. In an unsigned preface to James B. Connolly's *Fishermen of the Banks* (1928), Eliot wrote of how hard and dangerous the work was, and of how many widows the port had. The gear of foreign dead men may come from any ship wrecked off that coast.
