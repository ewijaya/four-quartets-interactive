The river's rhythm ran through the whole of Eliot's St Louis childhood, season by season: spring in the April dooryard, autumn at the table, winter round the gaslight. In 1930 he recalled being taken as a small boy to see the Eads Bridge when the river was in flood. The Eliots' house at 2635 Locust Street stood in a neighbourhood that, as he remembered it, was already growing shabby.
