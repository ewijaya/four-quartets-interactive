Without the Incarnation, action would only be movement — the motion of something that is moved without being its own source of movement, driven by ‘daemonic, chthonic / Powers’. A daemon in Greek thought is a spirit or divine force; chthonic means of the earth and the underworld. The language of movers and moved recalls Aristotle's unmoved mover, already present behind Burnt Norton's still point.

Right action, by contrast, is freedom from past and future — which for most of us is ‘the aim / Never here to be realised’.
