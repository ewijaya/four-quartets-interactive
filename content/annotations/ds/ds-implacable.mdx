The chiming of ‘ever’ and ‘however’ inside the line jars on purpose. Both Geoffrey Faber and John Hayward objected to internal rhymes of this kind in the draft, and Eliot kept them, calling the jolt deliberate, a hiccup in the right place. The river is implacable, not to be appeased: St Louis was flooded in 1892 and 1903, and the great Mississippi flood of 1927 was one of the worst natural disasters in American history.
