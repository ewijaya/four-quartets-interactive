The prayer's list of those at sea follows Psalm 107: they that go down to the sea in ships, that do business in great waters. The plain, almost official wording (those in the fish trade, those engaged in any lawful traffic) is deliberate: the prayer covers the fishing fleet, the merchant navy and the owners who direct them.
