The words repeat, exactly, a line near the end of Burnt Norton, where they were followed by ‘ridiculous the waste sad time’. There the urgency of the bird — ‘Quick, said the bird’ — led to a sense of all the wasted time around the moment. Here it leads instead to a condition of complete simplicity. The same words, heard again at the end of the journey, mean something new: the place known for the first time.
