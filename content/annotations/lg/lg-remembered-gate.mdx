The gate is the first gate of *Burnt Norton*, which led into the rose-garden: remembered, because the sequence began there, and unknown, because it was never gone through. What was last to be discovered is the beginning; Ricks and McCue recall that the last shall be first (Matthew 20:16). Kipling's story ‘They’, in which the ghosts of dead children are glimpsed in a house and garden but never quite seen, lies behind both passages.
