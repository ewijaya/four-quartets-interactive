The fire of Dante's *Purgatorio*, which cleanses rather than punishes. At the end of canto XXVI the Provençal poet Arnaut Daniel, having spoken to Dante in his own language, ‘hid himself in the fire that refines them’. Eliot had quoted the Italian line at the end of *The Waste Land* (1922) and used Arnaut's words as the title of his 1920 volume *Ara Vos Prec*.

In the fire the soul has to ‘move in measure, like a dancer’: purgation is a dance, not a torment endured passively. The image joins the fire and dance motifs and prepares for the last line of the sequence.
