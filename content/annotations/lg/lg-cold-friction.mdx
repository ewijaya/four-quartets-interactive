The first gift is the failing of the senses. In *Paradise Lost* (book XI) Michael warns Adam that in old age his senses will be dulled and all taste of pleasure forgone; in book X the fallen angels chew bitter ashes that looked like fruit, the fruit of shadows. Ricks and McCue also cite Conrad's *Youth*, with its sense of lost strength.
