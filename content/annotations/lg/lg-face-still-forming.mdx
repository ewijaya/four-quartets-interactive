In *Purgatorio* XXIII Dante cannot recognise his friend Forese Donati, whose face is wasted by the fasting of the gluttons, until he hears his voice. So here the words come first, and recognition follows. Eliot explained ‘preceded’: one knows it is someone one knows before one can say who.
