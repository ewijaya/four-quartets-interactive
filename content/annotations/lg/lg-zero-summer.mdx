John Hayward asked whether ‘zero summer’ alluded to the absolute zero of physics, and wondered whether the phrase was too much in the conceited manner of John Cleveland, the seventeenth-century metaphysical poet. Eliot kept it. A draft had a further line calling it a summer beyond sense, which could not be grasped; it was dropped late, perhaps because the paradox works better unexplained.
