We die with the dying and depart with them; born with the dead, we are brought back when they return. The rose's moment and the yew's, love's and death's, last equally long. A people that forgets its history cannot be saved from time, because history is a pattern made of timeless moments. So, as daylight fades on a winter afternoon in an out-of-the-way chapel, history is now, and it is England.
