Whatever the route or the season, you would have to lay aside the senses and your ideas. You are not here to check facts, to educate yourself, to satisfy curiosity or to bring back a report. You are here to kneel in a place where prayer has been real and effective. Eliot later said that prayer does not need a special place to be valid, but that a place where it has long been made keeps something of it. And prayer is more than an arrangement of words, more than the conscious work of a mind at prayer, or the sound of a voice praying.
