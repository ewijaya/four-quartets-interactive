The dove is the Holy Spirit descending at Pentecost with tongues of flame, and it is also the bomber of the second movement breaking the air with incendiary terror. The tongues ‘declare’ the only discharge from sin and error — a word that means both release from a debt and the firing of a weapon.

The choice is not between fire and no fire, but between two fires: the pyre of destruction or the pyre of purgation. We are ‘redeemed from fire by fire’.
