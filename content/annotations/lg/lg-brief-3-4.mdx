Why celebrate these dead more than the dying? Not to ring the bells backwards, the old alarm signal, nor to conjure up the ghost of a lost cause: the capitalised Rose, Eliot said, stands for King Charles the Martyr, and Gardner hears in the phrase Nijinsky's ballet *Le Spectre de la Rose*. Old factions and policies cannot be revived, and we cannot march to an old-fashioned drum. These men, those who fought them and those they fought, have all accepted the rule of silence and are gathered into one party. What we inherit from the winners we have taken from the losers, and what they could leave us is a symbol, made perfect by death.
