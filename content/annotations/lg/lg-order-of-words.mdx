Prayer is more than words, more than the mind's conscious attention, more than the voice. Eliot marked a passage in the *Spiritual Letters* of Dom John Chapman, a Benedictine abbot, which tells a troubled correspondent that when the word ‘God’ seems to mean nothing in prayer, that is no sign that one is on the wrong road. For a poet, whose work is an order of words, the admission is a large one.
