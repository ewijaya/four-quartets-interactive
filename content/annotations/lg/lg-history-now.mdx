Daylight is fading, one winter afternoon, in a secluded chapel — the church at Little Gidding, where the poem began in midwinter spring. At that moment ‘history is now and England’: the past is present, and the timeless moment has a place.

The phrase answers the first movement's ‘England and nowhere’. In 1942 the word England carried the weight of a country at war; the chapel is where that history and the other history, of prayer, meet.
