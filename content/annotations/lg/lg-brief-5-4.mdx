We will go on exploring, and at the end of all our exploring we will arrive where we began and see it, as if for the first time. As a young philosopher Eliot had written that true philosophy brings us back to the point we started from, which is the same and yet not the same. We come through the gate we do not know and yet remember, when the only part of the earth still undiscovered is the place we set out from: at the head of the longest river, the sound of a hidden waterfall, the children in the apple-tree, unknown because unsought, heard or half-heard in the quiet between two waves. The images gather up the garden of *Burnt Norton* and the rivers and seas of the later quartets.
