In his copy of Coleridge, Eliot marked the lines in ‘Frost at Midnight’ about tufts of snow on the bare branch of a mossy apple tree. Housman's cherry is hung with snow in ‘Loveliest of trees’. Geoffrey Faber doubted whether snow could be compared with May blossom; Eliot kept the comparison, which prepares for the real May blossom of the hedges in the next paragraph.
