In his recording Eliot stresses ‘perfected’ on the second syllable. A draft of the passage had lines on the damaged crown found in a thornbush, from the legend that Richard III's crown was found in a hawthorn after Bosworth, and on the Duke of Wellington's iron shutters, put up at Apsley House after a mob smashed his windows during the Reform crisis. Both are symbols made by defeat. Hope Mirrlees remembered Eliot wearing a white rose on the anniversary of Bosworth, in memory of Richard III, whom he regarded as the last English king.
