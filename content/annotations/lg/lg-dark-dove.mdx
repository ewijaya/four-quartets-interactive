The scene is London at dawn after an air raid. The ‘dark dove’, its tongue flickering, is a German bomber returning to base below the horizon, its tongue the flicker of its guns or of the fires it has left. Dead leaves rattle like tin on the asphalt; smoke rises from three districts.

The image is double. A dove is the traditional emblem of the Holy Spirit, and its tongue of flame recalls Pentecost. The fourth movement brings the two meanings together: the dove descending with ‘incandescent terror’ is both bomber and Spirit.
