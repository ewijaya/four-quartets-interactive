Donne, preaching at Christmas in 1622, spoke of the feast as falling at the dark time of the year. His ‘Nocturnal upon St Lucy's Day’, written for what was then the shortest day, finds the whole world's sap sunk to the ground; here, in the same season, the soul's sap stirs. Midwinter is when the Church celebrates the Nativity, a birth in the darkest time.
