In Matthew Arnold's ‘Stanzas from the Grande Chartreuse’ the poet wanders between two worlds, one dead and the other powerless to be born. Byron's *Don Juan* has life hovering between two worlds like a star, and Shelley's *Prometheus Unbound* speaks of two worlds of life and death. For the ghost, the passage between the worlds of the living and the dead has become easy, because in a city of fire and ruin the two have come to look alike.
