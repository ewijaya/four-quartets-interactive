The shortest fourth movement after Burnt Norton's: two stanzas of seven lines, each rhymed a b a b a c c, the closing couplet in each ending on ‘fire’. The strict, hymn-like form and the insistent rhymes give the lyric the force of a creed.

Across the four quartets the fourth movements make a sequence of their own: Burnt Norton's lyric of darkness and light, East Coker's Good Friday hymn, The Dry Salvages' prayer to the Virgin, and this lyric of Pentecost and Love.
