Memory is for liberation: love that expands beyond desire. In 1935 Eliot wrote of learning to love without desire, or beyond desire, drawing on St John of the Cross, who teaches that attachment to any created thing, even a book or a garment, hinders the soul's union with God. Donne's ‘Valediction: Forbidding Mourning’ turns separation into an expansion rather than a breach. Detachment here is not the absence of love but its enlargement.
