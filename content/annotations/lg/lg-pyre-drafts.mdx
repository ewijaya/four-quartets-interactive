In the first draft of 1941 this movement had two stanzas, the first beginning ‘Between the initial watery sign’, and John Hayward thought the section broke down. Eliot rebuilt it around the descending dove, which he said had to be an explicit reference to the Holy Spirit. Discarded words in the drafts include ‘crepitative’, ‘thanatos’ and ‘gilt-edged stocks and shares’.
