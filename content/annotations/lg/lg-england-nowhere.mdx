The movement ends by returning to The Dry Salvages' point where the timeless intersects with time and fixing it in a place. Here the intersection is ‘England and nowhere’, at once never and always. The paradox holds the two sides of the quartet together: a specific English place in wartime, and a moment that belongs to no place at all.

The phrase is echoed at the very end, where history is ‘now and England’.
