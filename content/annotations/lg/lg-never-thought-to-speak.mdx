In *Inferno* XXVII the damned Guido da Montefeltro speaks only because he believes that no one returns from hell to report his words. Eliot had used those lines as the epigraph to ‘The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock’ nearly thirty years before. Here a ghost speaks unexpectedly in streets he never expected to see again, and his words will be carried back to the living.
