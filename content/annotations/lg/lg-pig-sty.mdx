The approach is described with anti-romantic precision: a rough road, a turn behind a pig-sty, then the plain front of the little church, rebuilt in the early eighteenth century, and in front of its door the tomb of Nicholas Ferrar. Nothing is picturesque. The place matters not for what it looks like but for what was done there.
