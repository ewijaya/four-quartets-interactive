In Tennyson's *In Memoriam* (section VII) the blank day breaks on a bald street, after a night of grief. In *Purgatorio* XVI Marco Lombardo turns back when he sees the light shining through the smoke. The ghost leaves as ghosts must, at daybreak, with a kind of blessing.
