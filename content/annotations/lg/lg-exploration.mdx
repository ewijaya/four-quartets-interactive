Perhaps the most quoted lines Eliot wrote: the end of all our exploring will be to arrive where we started and know the place for the first time. East Coker said old men ought to be explorers and that home is where one starts from; here the voyage comes round.

What is found at the end is the beginning: through the ‘unknown, unremembered gate’ — the first gate of Burnt Norton's garden — back to what was always there but never truly known.
