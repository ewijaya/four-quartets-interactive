A line from the second chapter of *The Cloud of Unknowing*, the anonymous fourteenth-century English treatise on contemplative prayer, where the author speaks of the heart being woken by love's drawing and by its calling voice. Eliot sets it apart, as a single line, like a phrase heard from outside the poem.

It gives the exploration that follows its motive: we do not cease because we are drawn and called. The ‘Love’ is the same Love named in the fourth movement.
