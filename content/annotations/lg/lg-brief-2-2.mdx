Flood and drought both come over the eyes and into the mouth, as dead water and dead sand fight for mastery. The parched soil, gutted and exhausted, gapes at the futility of human labour and laughs without joy: earth, too, has died. In *The Idea of a Christian Society* (1939) Eliot had warned that exhausting the earth's natural resources for profit ends in scarcity and desert.
