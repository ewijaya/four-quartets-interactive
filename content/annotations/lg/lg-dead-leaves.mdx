In Shelley's ‘Ode to the West Wind’ dead leaves are driven like ghosts fleeing an enchanter. Eliot said that the image of the ghost blown towards the speaker like the metal leaves would have been impossible without his reading of the *Inferno*. The leaves that rattle like tin are real leaves, and perhaps also the fragments of metal left on the streets after a raid.
