Julian's promise returns: all will be well, through the purifying of motive at the very ground of prayer. In Julian's final revelation Christ calls himself the ground of her beseeching, the source of prayer and not only its object. Eliot said he turned to Julian and *The Cloud of Unknowing* so that fourteenth-century voices would keep the passage free of sentimental nostalgia for the seventeenth century. The section once ended with a version of the old eucharistic prayer *Anima Christi*, which he cut.
