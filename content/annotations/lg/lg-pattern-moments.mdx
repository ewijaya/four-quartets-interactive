The choruses of *The Rock* (1934) had spoken of a moment not out of time but in time, in what we call history. ‘Tradition and the Individual Talent’ (1919) describes the historical sense as a sense of the timeless as well as the temporal, and of the two together. History, in this view, is not a sequence but a pattern, made of moments that belong both to their time and to no time.
