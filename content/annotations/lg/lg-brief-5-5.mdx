Quick now, here and now, always: a state of complete simplicity, which costs everything. And all will be well, and every kind of thing will be well, when the flames' tongues fold inward into one crowned knot of fire, and fire and rose are one. The ending recalls Dante's final vision in the *Paradiso*, where the scattered leaves of the universe are bound by love into one volume, and the great rose of the blessed. The fire of suffering and purging and the rose of love are shown to be one.
