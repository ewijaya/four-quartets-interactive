The verse changes to long unrhymed lines in imitation of Dante's terza rima. It is the uncertain hour before dawn, after the last enemy bomber, the dark dove of the poem, has gone home and before the All Clear has sounded, at the end of an endless night. The speaker is an air-raid warden on his round in Kensington. Dead leaves rattle like tin over the empty road between three districts where smoke is rising. He meets someone walking, both idling and hurrying, blown towards him like the metal leaves by the city's dawn wind. As he stares into the lowered face, the scene follows Dante's meeting with his old teacher Brunetto Latini among the dead in the *Inferno*.
