Love devised the torment. At the end of her *Revelations* Julian of Norwich learns that love was the Lord's meaning. In *Inferno* V Francesca, blown on the winds of the lustful, begins her story with love, which is quickly kindled in the gentle heart. *Ash-Wednesday* had spoken of the torment of love unsatisfied, and the greater torment of love satisfied. A draft asked who had heaped the brittle rose leaves, recalling the rose petals let fall from church roofs at Pentecost in Italy.
