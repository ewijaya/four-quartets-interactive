Images from all four quartets are gathered: the source of the longest river (recalling the river of The Dry Salvages), the hidden waterfall, the children in the apple-tree, the stillness between two waves. The children, heard but not seen, are those who were ‘hidden excitedly’ in the leaves of Burnt Norton's garden; Eliot's short poem ‘New Hampshire’ (1934) had already heard children's voices in an orchard and ended in an apple-tree.

They are ‘not known, because not looked for’: the moment comes only when it is not sought.
