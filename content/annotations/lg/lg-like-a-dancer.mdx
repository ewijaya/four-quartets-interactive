In the first revision the ghost said that in the refining fire one must learn to swim, recalling the souls in *Purgatorio* XXVI who move in the fire like fish in water. Eliot replaced it with the dancer, which joins the fire to the dance of the whole sequence. Sir John Davies's *Orchestra* (1596) defines dancing as moving in measure. Eliot also remembered Antony's scornful remark in *Antony and Cleopatra* that Octavius at Philippi kept his sword like a dancer.
