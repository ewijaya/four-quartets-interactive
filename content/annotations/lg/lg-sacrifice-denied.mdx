On the night of 29 December 1940 incendiary bombs burned out much of the City of London, including a number of Wren's churches, and the water poured on the fires soaked whatever did not burn. Eliot had cared about the City churches for twenty years: in 1921 he wrote in protest against a proposal to demolish nineteen of them, which he valued as places of refuge. The sacrifice denied may be the one that faith asks for, which a comfortable nation had declined.
