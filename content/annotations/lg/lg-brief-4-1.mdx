Eliot called this lyric his Pentecost poem. A dove comes down, splitting the air with the flame of white-hot terror, and its tongues of fire proclaim the only release from sin and error. It is the Holy Spirit descending as at Pentecost; after the dark dove of the second movement, it is also a bomber diving on the city. The one hope, or else despair, is in choosing which fire to burn in: to be saved from fire by fire.
