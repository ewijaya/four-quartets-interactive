Another phrase from Julian of Norwich. In the fourteenth showing Christ tells her that he is the ground of her beseeching: the very desire to pray comes from God, who both prompts the prayer and answers it. All shall be well, the poem says, by the purification of the motive — not by any change in circumstance, but by a change in why we act.
