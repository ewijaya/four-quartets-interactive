The use of memory is liberation: not less love, but love expanding beyond desire, which frees us from future and past alike. So love of one's country begins as attachment to one's own sphere of action, and learns that action matters little, though it never becomes a matter of indifference. Burke had argued that loving the small group we belong to is the first step towards loving our country and mankind. History can enslave, or it can set free. Now the faces and places vanish, with the self that loved them as well as it could, to be renewed and transfigured in another pattern.
