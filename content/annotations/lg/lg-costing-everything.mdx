A condition of complete simplicity, and the price in brackets: not less than everything. The parenthesis is the whole teaching of St John of the Cross in five words — to come to possess everything, desire to possess nothing — and it recalls East Coker's way of dispossession. Simplicity is not easy; it is the end of a long stripping-away.

Then Julian's words return — all shall be well — and the sequence moves to its last image.
