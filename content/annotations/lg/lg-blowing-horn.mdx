Day breaks, and the ghost leaves ‘with a kind of valediction’ and fades as the horn sounds. The line recalls the first scene of *Hamlet*, where the ghost of the dead king vanishes at cock-crow: it ‘faded on the crowing of the cock’. In 1940s London the horn is also the siren sounding the all-clear at the end of a raid.

Like Hamlet's ghost, this one appears to men on watch at night, speaks of the sins of his life, and departs at dawn.
