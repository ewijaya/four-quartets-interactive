Three states that grow in the same hedgerow: attachment to self, things and persons; detachment from them; and, between them, indifference, which resembles both as death resembles life. The image is botanical. The dead-nettle is a common plant that looks like the stinging nettle but has no sting — alive, but lacking what made the other formidable.

Detachment is not coldness. Memory is for liberation: not less love, but love expanding beyond desire, and so freed from future and past. Love of one's country begins as attachment to one's own field of action and grows into something wider.
