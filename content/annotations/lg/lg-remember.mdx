The ghost of Hamlet's father leaves his son with the command to remember him, and returns in the closet scene to whet his almost blunted purpose. Eliot's ghost reverses this: he does not want his old thought and theory remembered, and asks forgiveness for his own words and prays that the speaker's will be forgiven. The *Hamlet* echo returns at the end of the passage, when the ghost fades at the horn.
