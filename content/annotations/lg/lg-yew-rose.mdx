The rose — love, the rose-garden, the moment of illumination — and the yew — the churchyard tree, death — are ‘of equal duration’. Neither moment lasts longer or matters more; both belong to the pattern. Burnt Norton's yew curled its fingers down in the dark; The Dry Salvages ended not far from it.

A people that forgets its history cannot be redeemed from time, because history is a pattern of such timeless moments.
