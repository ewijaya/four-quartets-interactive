The Ferrar household was not a gathering of saints. Bernard Blackstone's work on the Ferrar papers in the 1930s brought out family quarrels, and in particular the difficult behaviour of Bathsheba Ferrar, the wife of Nicholas's brother John. They were not the poet's kin, and not especially kind: Ricks and McCue hear Hamlet's first line, a little more than kin and less than kind. Yet some had peculiar genius, and all were touched by the genius of the place.
