The cry translates Dante's words on recognising Brunetto Latini among the sodomites in the seventh circle of Hell: *Siete voi qui, ser Brunetto?* — ‘Are you here, Ser Brunetto?’ Eliot drops the name, so that the recognition is left without an object: the speaker knows the ghost, and cannot say who he is.
