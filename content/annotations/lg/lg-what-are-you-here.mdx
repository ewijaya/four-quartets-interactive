The cry translates Dante's words on recognising Brunetto Latini among the sodomites in the seventh circle of Hell: *Siete voi qui, ser Brunetto?* — ‘Are you here, Ser Brunetto?’ The drafts kept the name, and Eliot removed it in August 1942: he did not want to place Yeats in Hell, or to impute Brunetto's sin to anyone, and he wanted the effect to be purgatorial. Eliot drops the name, so that the recognition is left without an object: the speaker knows the ghost, and cannot say who he is.
