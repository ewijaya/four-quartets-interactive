Day is breaking. In the damaged street the ghost takes his leave with a kind of blessing and fades at the sounding of a horn, as the ghost of Hamlet's father fades at cockcrow. Hayward heard in the horn the All Clear siren that ended an air raid. Eliot kept the echo of *Hamlet* on purpose, although Hayward objected to it.
