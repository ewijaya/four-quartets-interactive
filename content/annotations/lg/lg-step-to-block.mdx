Any action is a step towards death, and the four deaths named recall the four quartets' worlds: the block of the executed king, the fire of martyrs and of the Blitz, the sea's throat that swallowed the drowned of The Dry Salvages, and an illegible gravestone like the ‘old stones that cannot be deciphered’ in East Coker. ‘And that is where we start.’

The dead depart and we go with them; they return and bring us back with them. Past and present are joined in a single movement.
