The dead can tell us, now they are dead, what they had no words for while they lived. What they tell us is touched with fire, like the tongues of flame that came down on the apostles at Pentecost, and it goes beyond the language of the living. Eliot remarked that we cannot fully understand a person until after their death, when their actions fall into perspective. Here, where the timeless moment crosses time, is England and yet nowhere, never and always.
