Eliot told a correspondent in 1961 that the ghost was primarily Yeats, and this line is the plainest trace of him. Yeats died at Roquebrune-Cap-Martin in the south of France in January 1939 and was buried there; in 1948 his body was brought back to Drumcliff in County Sligo. John Hayward also recalled the shades in the *Aeneid* (book VI) stretching out their hands in longing for the farther shore.
