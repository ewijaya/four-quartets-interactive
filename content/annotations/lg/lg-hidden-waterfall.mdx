In *The Family Reunion* (1939) Harry recalls the distant waterfall in the forest, inaccessible, half-heard, and a silence between two storms. The waterfall was also one of the unattended moments listed in *The Dry Salvages* V, and the longest river recalls the Mississippi of that quartet. John Hayward suggested ‘stillness’ in place of ‘silence’ between the waves of the sea, and Eliot hesitated before accepting it.
