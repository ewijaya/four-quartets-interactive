The midwinter scene had been in Eliot's mind for years. A chorus in *Murder in the Cathedral* (1935) speaks of spring come in winter, snow in the branches floating like blossom, and ice along the ditches mirroring the sun. Ricks and McCue hear Walter Pater's Conclusion to *The Renaissance*, with its short day of frost and sun, and Shelley's *The Triumph of Life*, whose cold glare blinds. The sun on the ice is fire in frost: the first of the quartet's many fires.
