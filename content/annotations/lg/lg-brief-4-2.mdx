Who invented the torment? Love is the unfamiliar name of the one who wove the unbearable shirt of flame, which no human power can take off, like the poisoned tunic that burned Hercules to death. We live and breathe only to be consumed by one fire or the other, the fire of selfish desire or the fire of divine love.
