The last movement opens by gathering the sequence's formulas about beginnings and ends: Burnt Norton's end that precedes the beginning, East Coker's motto in both of its forms. Here they are stated calmly, almost as common sense: ending is beginning, and the end is our starting point.

The rest of the movement enacts the statement, returning to the first quartet's garden and gate at the moment of ending.
