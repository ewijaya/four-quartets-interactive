What you thought you came for proves to be only a shell around a meaning, and the purpose breaks out of it only when it is fulfilled, if it ever is. Perhaps there was no purpose; or else it lies beyond the end you imagined, and is changed in being fulfilled. Other places are also the world's end. John Hayward, Eliot's friend and adviser, suggested the island monasteries of Iona and Lindisfarne on the edge of the sea, St Kevin's retreat by the lake at Glendalough, the Egyptian desert of the first hermits, and a city such as Padua. But this place is the closest, in space and in time: here, now, in England.
